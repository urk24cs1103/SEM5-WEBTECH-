2. Basic Financial Terms

Here are a few terms that every beginner should learn:

Budget
A plan for tracking how much money comes in and goes out each month.
Stock
A small ownership share in a company.
Interest
Money earned over time on money stored in a savings account.

3. Guides for Beginners

Steps to Start Saving (Ordered List)

  1. Make a monthly budget
    1. Write down income
    2. Track daily spending
  2. Open a savings account
    1. Choose a good bank
    2. Deposit money regularly

Types of Investments (Unordered List)

  • Low Risk Options
    • High-Yield Savings Accounts
    • Government Bonds
  • Higher Risk Options
    • Company Stocks
    • Index Funds

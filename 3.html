Asset allocation is the cornerstone of risk management in personal finance. Spreading capital across equities, debt instruments, and real estate protects portfolios against severe market swings while supporting long-term growth.

Core Asset Categories:

  • Equity Mutual Funds & Index ETFs
  • Fixed Income Securities & Bonds
  • Real Estate & Physical Commodities

2. The Power of Compound Interest

Compound interest allows investments to generate returns on previous gains in addition to the principal amount. Over extended periods, compounding creates exponential growth that outpaces traditional savings accounts.

Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) is an ideal strategy to leverage compounding. Investing a fixed amount regularly eliminates market timing risks and lowers the average purchase price over time.
